Common questions

How do I add my child?

Open Family, enter their name and phone number, choose Child and tap Text invite. When they sign in to Oppry with that number, they join your family and you can manage their tasks.

Why don't I see the tasks a parent added for me?

Make sure you signed in with the phone number the invite was sent to, and that you have the latest version of Oppry. Tasks arrive within about 30 seconds while the app is open.

How do reminders work on Apple Watch?

Install Oppry on your Apple Watch from the Watch app on your iPhone. It shows your iPhone's tasks for today, and reminders tap your wrist with Done and In 10 min buttons. What you tick on the watch shows up on your iPhone, and the other way round.

How do I delete my account?

Open Family → Account → Delete account. This deletes your tasks and history and takes you out of your family. It can't be undone.
