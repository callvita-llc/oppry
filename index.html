Family routines, without the nagging

Parents set up tasks and reminders for their kids: drink water, brush teeth, read, chores. Kids tap Done on their phone or Apple Watch, earn points, and parents see how the day is going.

  • Repeating reminders with Done and In 10 min buttons
  • A family view: see who's on track and who's behind
  • Points for tasks, set by a parent
  • Water goals, saved to Apple Health from Apple Watch
  • Describe a task in your own words, and AI drafts it for you
