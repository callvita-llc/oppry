Privacy Policy

Oppry, by Callvita LLC

OpprySupport

Last updated: November 5, 2026

In short

  • We collect what Oppry needs to work: your sign-in details, your family, and your tasks.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time in the app: Family → Account → Delete account.

What we collect

Your account

Your phone number, used to sign in with a texted code, and the name you give when you sign up. If you sign in with an existing Callvita email account, we use that email address.

Your family

Your family's name, each member's display name and role (parent or child), and the phone numbers a parent enters to invite someone.

Your tasks

Tasks and reminders (titles, icons, schedules, points), each time a task is marked done or undone, the amount of water logged with water tasks, and a log of changes (who added, changed or completed a task). Parents in a family can see and edit their children's tasks.

What you type into "Describe a task"

If you use AI to draft tasks, we keep the request and the drafted tasks, and count requests to apply a daily limit.

Health data

On Apple Watch, Oppry saves each drink you log to Apple Health as water, and reads today's water total from Apple Health to show it to you. On Android, Oppry does the same with Health Connect. Oppry asks your permission first, and you can change it at any time in the Health app or Health Connect.

Data Oppry reads from Apple Health or Health Connect stays on your device. We don't send it to our servers, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone. The water amounts of the tasks you mark done in Oppry are stored with your tasks, as described above.

Who else handles your data

We use these service providers, only to run Oppry:

  • Amazon Web Services (Amazon Cognito): sign-in, and the texted sign-in codes.
  • ClickSend: sends the invite text when a parent invites someone by phone number.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI): drafts tasks when you use "Describe a task". It receives what you type, the names in your family and their current task titles. Oppry asks before sending anything to it, and you can use the app without it.

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, we delete your tasks, history, points, activity, AI requests and the invite you joined with, and take you out of your family (the family itself is deleted when its last member leaves). Your sign-in is deleted too, unless the same login is also used for the Callvita app.

To ask for deletion another way, or for a copy of your data, email support@callvita.com.

Children

Oppry is built for families: a parent sets it up and invites their children, and parents can see and manage their children's tasks. If you're under 13, a parent should set up Oppry for you. If you believe a child has given us information without a parent's consent, email support@callvita.com and we'll delete it.

Security

Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections, and only signed-in family members can see a family's data.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
